# Sponsorships and donations

The Planetary Atlas may accept sponsorship or donations to support its public-interest work: maintaining the catalogue, preserving provenance, improving accessibility, and building open research tools.

No donation channel is active yet.

## Principles

- Sponsorship does not buy editorial control, preferred treatment of a mission, target, dataset, or scientific conclusion.
- Funding must not affect how evidence, uncertainty, source attribution, or modelled visualisations are presented.
- Donor acknowledgement is opt-in. We will not publish names, amounts, or contact information without permission.
- We will clearly identify any organisation that funds a distinct feature, dataset integration, or event.
- We will not imply endorsement by NASA, IPAC, Caltech, DESI, Gaia, JWST, TESS, or any other data provider.

## Before enabling donations

Before publishing a funding link, maintainers must select a provider, publish the relevant legal and tax wording for the receiving entity, define refunds and chargeback handling, set up a private contact route, and test the full donation journey. Provider fees, data handling, geographic availability, and public display options must be reviewed.

## Prospective uses of funds

- Reliable hosting, backups, monitoring, and data refreshes.
- Accessibility, performance, and security improvements.
- Data-source and mission-product integrations.
- Open documentation, scientific-method review, and public educational material.

Funding does not guarantee any specific deliverable or timeline.

## Contact

Until a formal sponsorship channel is published, contact the repository owner through the repository's existing private contact route. Do not send payment details, credentials, or personal financial information through GitHub issues.
